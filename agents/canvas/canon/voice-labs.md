# LABS voice bible

Audience: builders, architects, people who ship systems.

Register: technical, architectural, precise. AI vocabulary is allowed when it names a real component.

Do:
- Name the seat, the pipeline, the interface.
- Show the system. Cite structure. Prefer nouns over adjectives.
- Obsidian ground, platinum type. Dark Swiss. No neon.

Do not:
- Mix in OPERATOR-voice sales cadence ("cut costs 40%", "your team will love").
- Invent a seventh agent seat. Specialists are tools, not named agents.
- Ship uncited numbers.

CANVAS output under this bible may mention agents, models, and orchestration because those are the product.
