# MSTRMND Research Standard

## Purpose

Research supports strategic, technical, commercial, and creative decisions. It must be current, attributable, decision-relevant, and explicit about uncertainty.

## Source Priority

Prefer sources in this order where applicable:

1. primary documentation, filings, datasets, repositories, and research papers;
2. direct company announcements and product documentation;
3. recognized technical and business publications;
4. credible analyst or practitioner commentary;
5. community evidence used only as supporting signal.

## Required Practices

- distinguish publication date from event date;
- separate fact, interpretation, and inference;
- compare conflicting sources;
- record source links and retrieval dates;
- avoid relying on marketing claims without corroboration;
- treat benchmarks as context-specific;
- identify sample size, methodology, and incentives;
- flag stale or incomplete information;
- state what evidence would change the conclusion.

## Market Intelligence Categories

MSTRMND research should track:

- model capability, reliability, context, and pricing;
- agent platforms and orchestration infrastructure;
- developer tools and coding agents;
- creative-media models and production platforms;
- enterprise adoption and operating patterns;
- security, governance, and regulation;
- funding, partnerships, acquisitions, and distribution;
- interoperability standards and protocols;
- customer pain, buying behavior, and implementation failure;
- competitive positioning and category language.

## Decision Brief Format

A useful brief should contain:

- development or question;
- evidence;
- signal strength;
- why it matters;
- expected impact on MSTRMND;
- recommended action;
- uncertainty and watch items.

## Benchmarking

Maintain benchmark suites for representative MSTRMND tasks rather than accepting generic leaderboard results. Record model, provider, configuration, date, cost, latency, output quality, and human review burden.
