# MSTRMND Offers and Pricing

## Commercial Strategy

Sell the operational transformation first and the platform second.

Most companies will not initially ask for an agentic intelligence layer. They will recognize symptoms:

- disconnected tools and data
- repetitive manual work
- dropped opportunities
- inconsistent execution
- knowledge trapped in people and documents
- AI pilots that never became operational
- rising software and model spend
- no governance or visibility

MSTRMND should diagnose these conditions, solve one high-value operational loop, prove measurable value, and expand into the company's managed intelligence layer.

## Offer Ladder

### 1. MSTRMND Blueprint

A paid diagnostic and architecture engagement.

#### Includes

- business vision and priority alignment
- software, data, model, and workflow inventory
- operational bottleneck analysis
- AI readiness and risk assessment
- tool and subscription redundancy review
- initial agent and skill architecture
- three prioritized implementation opportunities
- projected value, cost, and implementation sequence
- KPI and governance framework
- 90-day roadmap

#### Deliverables

- current-state systems map
- future-state orchestration architecture
- opportunity matrix
- implementation roadmap
- recommended models, tools, integrations, and skills
- governance and measurement plan

#### Indicative Pricing

- small business: $2,500–$5,000
- growth company: $7,500–$15,000
- complex organization: $20,000+

Discovery can be free. The strategic design work should be paid.

### 2. MSTRMND Launch

The first production implementation centered on one high-value operating loop.

#### Example Loops

- inbound lead to qualified appointment
- proposal creation to follow-up
- support request to resolution
- content strategy to publishing and analysis
- client onboarding to project activation
- internal request to approved execution
- software issue to tested pull request

#### Includes

- one production operational intelligence system
- integrations with existing tools
- company-specific knowledge and context
- model routing
- reusable skills
- approvals and escalation rules
- observability and cost controls
- operator dashboard
- team training
- initial optimization period

#### Indicative Pricing

- focused SMB implementation: $12,000–$25,000
- multi-system growth implementation: $25,000–$60,000
- complex department deployment: $60,000–$150,000+

Sell the measurable operating outcome, not a count of agents or automations.

### 3. MSTRMND Core

The recurring managed intelligence-layer relationship.

#### Recurring Scope

- orchestration infrastructure
- provider and model management
- tool and MCP integrations
- context and knowledge maintenance
- workflow monitoring
- skill registry
- permissions and governance
- cost and performance optimization
- evaluation and quality assurance
- system improvements
- strategic operator reviews
- incident response and support

#### Suggested Service Levels

##### Core

For one or two operational systems.

Indicative fee: $3,000–$6,000 per month.

Includes managed orchestration, bounded workflows and integrations, monitoring, monthly optimization, cost reporting, and standard support.

##### Scale

For multiple workflows or departments.

Indicative fee: $8,000–$15,000 per month.

Includes broader orchestration, custom skills, centralized company knowledge, advanced analytics, weekly optimization, governance, priority support, and quarterly architecture planning.

##### Strategic

For organizations treating agentic systems as core infrastructure.

Indicative fee: $20,000–$50,000+ per month.

Includes company-wide architecture, multiple business units, specialized model options, dedicated operator support, custom evaluations, security and compliance alignment, continuous workflow development, executive reporting, and transformation leadership.

### 4. MSTRMND Expansion

Expand by operational domain, not by adding disconnected bots.

Potential domains:

- Revenue Intelligence
- Customer Operations
- Creative Intelligence
- Engineering Intelligence
- Executive Intelligence
- Finance and Administration
- Knowledge and Training
- Field Operations

Every expansion should connect to the same context, policies, identity, observability, and governance layers.

## Entry Offers

### AI Systems and Spend Audit

For companies already paying for several AI tools without a coherent architecture.

Promise:

> Identify where the AI stack is wasting money, duplicating work, creating risk, or failing to connect with measurable business outcomes.

### Revenue Operations Intelligence

For service businesses with lead response, qualification, follow-up, proposal, and booking problems.

Promise:

> Create a connected revenue system that captures, qualifies, follows up, books, and preserves context across the customer journey.

### Closed-Loop Content Engine

For founder-led brands, agencies, entertainment companies, and multi-location businesses.

Promise:

> Turn company intelligence into an always-on content operation that plans, produces, distributes, measures, and improves creative work.

### Agentic Engineering System

For software teams.

Promise:

> Connect coding agents, repositories, testing, documentation, issue tracking, and human approval into one governed engineering system.

## Commercial Structure

Every agreement should separate:

### Strategy and Architecture

One-time Blueprint or systems-design fee.

### Implementation

One-time build, integration, testing, and deployment fee.

### Platform and Management

Recurring managed intelligence-layer fee.

### Consumption

Model, voice, media generation, storage, search, data, and third-party API usage.

### Expansion

New domains, workflows, skills, integrations, departments, and capabilities.

This structure prevents retainers from becoming unlimited custom development and keeps consumption costs transparent.

## Ideal Customer Profile

Strong early customers typically:

- have 10–100 employees
- use five or more SaaS systems
- experience operational fragmentation directly
- spend meaningfully on labor, agencies, software, or content
- have recurring workflows with measurable outputs
- are already experimenting with AI
- lack an internal AI platform team
- can make decisions without enterprise-length procurement

Best early buyers are often AI-fragmented operators rather than complete AI beginners.

Potential verticals:

- professional services
- agencies and production companies
- real estate and finance operators
- logistics and field services
- legal and healthcare-adjacent administration
- founder-led ecommerce
- media and entertainment
- small software companies

## Value Language

Do not lead with:

- number of agents
- number of prompts
- API connections
- automation hours
- token usage
- chatbots
- digital employees

Lead with:

- operational coherence
- faster execution
- preserved institutional knowledge
- improved response time
- fewer dropped opportunities
- lower coordination burden
- increased output without equivalent headcount growth
- consistent decision-making
- vendor flexibility
- measurable system performance
- reduced fragmentation

## Packaging Rule

The master positioning may be broad. The first sale must be specific.

Start with one urgent, measurable operational problem. Use that implementation to establish the shared intelligence layer, then expand from proven value.
